Course Description

This course provides students with a solid understanding of the principles underlying the design and operation of computer networks. Following a top-down structure, key topics include network architectures, the World Wide Web and HTTP, transport-layer protocols (TCP and UDP), the Internet Protocol (IP) and routing, data link protocols, and wireless networks such as Wi-Fi and 5G.
